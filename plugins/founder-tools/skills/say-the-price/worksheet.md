# Say The Price — Worksheet

Fill this in before you start, or work through it live in the session. Either way
it takes about ten minutes.

Write short answers. Write the true ones. A worksheet filled in with what you wish
were true produces a number built on what you wish were true.

If you get stuck on question 3, stop filling this in. Question 3 is the whole
thing, and the answer is not in your head. It is in a conversation with someone
who has the problem.
