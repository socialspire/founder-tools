# The Free Thing

Part of **Getting Demand**. Version 1.0.0

Turns advertising money into units in people's hands, prices the giveaway before you promise it, and closes the exits where a prospect walks away with nothing.

## When to run it

Run it when your ads are getting more expensive, before you offer a free trial or sample, or when people take the free thing and disappear.

## What it gives you

The giveaway priced against what you currently pay to acquire a customer, the exits named and closed, and the ask that follows the free thing written out.

## What it will not do

It will not invent a number, a date, a price, a deadline or a claim about your
business, and nothing it drafts for a page or an offer carries a commercial term
you did not state. Where it needs a fact only you hold, it leaves a bracket and
asks for it at the end.

It reasons from a bank of verified cases that ships with the skill. Where the
library holds nothing on your situation it says so rather than filling the gap.

## Install

You need the marketplace added once. In Claude Code:

```bash
/plugin marketplace add socialspire/founder-tools
```

Then install the course:

```bash
/plugin install getting-demand@socialspire
```
