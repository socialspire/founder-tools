# The Repetition Ledger

Part of **Getting Demand**. Version 1.0.0

Rules whether the thing you want to change is decaying or you are just bored of it, writes the number that retires it, and decides what to run ten times instead of what to try next.

## When to run it

Run it when your team wants new creative, when a campaign feels tired, or when you are about to rebrand.

## What it gives you

A decay verdict read off your own numbers, a written retirement threshold you set before the next run, and a ruling on whether to repeat or replace.

## What it will not do

It will not invent a number, a date, a price, a deadline or a claim about your
business, and nothing it drafts for a page or an offer carries a commercial term
you did not state. Where it needs a fact only you hold, it leaves a bracket and
asks for it at the end.

It reasons from a bank of verified cases that ships with the skill. Where the
library holds nothing on your situation it says so rather than filling the gap.
