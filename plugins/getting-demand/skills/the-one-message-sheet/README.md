# The One Message Sheet

Part of **Getting Demand**. Version 1.0.0

Counts how many different things your marketing is saying, rules which single one survives on the numbers attached to it, and names who that message is actually written for.

## When to run it

Run it when you cannot say in one sentence what you sell, when six assets say six things, or when you are about to write something new before deciding what the old ones proved.

## What it gives you

A count of every distinct claim across what you pasted, one surviving message ruled on the evidence, the number that would settle it if none of them carry one yet, and the reader that message is written for.

## What it will not do

It will not invent a number, a date, a price, a deadline or a claim about your
business, and nothing it drafts for a page or an offer carries a commercial term
you did not state. Where it needs a fact only you hold, it leaves a bracket and
asks for it at the end.

It reasons from a bank of verified cases that ships with the skill. Where the
library holds nothing on your situation it says so rather than filling the gap.
