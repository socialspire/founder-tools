# John D. Rockefeller — a skill for your AI

This installs a method. Once it is in place, you hand your AI a real piece of
your work and it runs Rockefeller's documented decision procedure on it, then
gives you back an artifact you can act on this week.

It does not roleplay him. It does not speak as him. More on why at the bottom.

## What is in the folder

```
john-d-rockefeller/
  SKILL.md
  README.md
  reference/
    deck.md
    procedures.md
```

Four files. That is the whole thing. It depends on nothing outside this folder.

## Install it

You install this one on its own, from its course in the classroom. Two minutes.
It does not touch any of the other four, and you can add them later or not at all.

## What to type

You do not need a special command. Bring it a real piece of work and name him.

- run Rockefeller on my pricing
- what would Rockefeller do with this landing page
- audit my costs
- I'm about to quit my job to do this full time, run Rockefeller
- I don't know if this is actually profitable

The first thing it does, every time, is read your situation back to you and ask
whether it has it right. Answer that honestly. It will not run until you
confirm, and that check is the difference between a useful artifact and a
confident wrong one.

Then it routes you to one of four procedures:

| What you bring | What you get |
|---|---|
| A price, a cost, a margin worry | A dated unit-cost sheet with one cost line removed |
| Drift, a stalled week | A one-page daily sheet you fill in for seven mornings |
| A market you want to enter | A ranked ledger of every operator serving your customer |
| A commitment you are about to make | A one-page campaign brief with a written trigger |

## A worked example

**What you type**

> run Rockefeller on this. I'm building a done-for-you Notion setup service for
> freelancers. Charging $300. Takes me about a day. No customers yet, three
> conversations that went nowhere.

**What comes back, first**

It writes your situation back in four lines and asks whether that is right. It
will probably note that you said about a day, and that about is doing a lot of
work in that sentence. You confirm or you correct it.

**What comes back, after you confirm**

It routes you to the cost audit, because you brought a price. Then it asks for
things you may not want to hand over: your actual time log for the last two
weeks, every subscription you pay for, the hours you spent on those three
conversations. If you send estimates instead, it asks again. That is the tool
working.

Then you get roughly this shape:

```
SITUATION (confirmed)
Pre-revenue. Done-for-you Notion setup, $300, sold to freelancers.
Three conversations, no close. Constraint is time and no proof.

PROCEDURE RUN
The Solder-Drop Cost Audit. You brought a price you have not measured.

THE ARTIFACT
UNIT COST SHEET — <date>
Unit: one Notion workspace built and handed over
Inputs, largest first:
  1. My build time, 9.5h at $40 — $380
  2. Pre-sale calls and scoping, 2.5h — $100
  3. Post-handover fixes, 1.5h — $60
  ...
Total cost per unit: <figure above your price>
Governing line: build time
What the category treats as fixed: every build starts from blank
One attack on it: build once as a template, sell the fill-in
Line removed this week: the second scoping call
New total: <figure> (was <figure>)

WHAT THE RECORD SAYS
Two or three principles, in the tool's own words, with the book named.

WHAT HE WOULD CUT
The part of your offer he would remove, stated plainly.

DO THIS WEEK
One action, starting with a verb.

WRONG IF
The condition that would prove the action wrong.

OUT OF REACH
The part of the method that does not transfer at your scale, and why.

REFERENCE DID NOT COVER
What you asked that the material does not reach.
```

The artifact is the point. Everything else is context around it.

## A note on sourcing

This skill reasons only from documented material about John D. Rockefeller: four
books that carry him directly, and four more that carry a single story about him
as a cross-reference. All eight are named inside the skill.

Three things follow from that, and you should know all three.

**It paraphrases. It never quotes.** The material is a distillation of
biographies and memoirs, not a transcript. Nobody involved has the words on a
page. So nothing this skill produces will put words in his mouth, and nothing
will appear inside quotation marks. That costs a little rhetorical force and
buys you the certainty that no citation in your hands is wrong.

**It will not use general knowledge.** There is a long list of famous
Rockefeller material the reference does not carry — the antitrust case, the
philanthropy, the exposé, the dates, the fortune. If you ask about any of it,
the skill will tell you the reference does not cover it and stop. That is the
correct behaviour, not a bug.

**It will tell you where the method ends.** A lot of what he did only works at
industrial scale. Where a move does not reach a founder with no customers, the
skill says so and gives you the part that does, or marks it out of reach. It
will not invent a small version of a big move to keep the answer tidy.

## One last thing

This skill does not tell you your idea is good. That is deliberate. It tells you
what is wrong with the work and what he would cut. If you want encouragement,
this is the wrong tool.
